01 - The Three Languages

Every Website Is Three Languages

HTML

What it is.

The words, the images, the links.

<h1>Your Name</h1>

CSS

What it looks like.

Color, size, spacing, position.

h1 { color: red; }

JavaScript

What it does.

Things that happen when you click.

alert("hi");
Today is mostly HTML. It is the part you cannot skip, and the part that is genuinely easy.
