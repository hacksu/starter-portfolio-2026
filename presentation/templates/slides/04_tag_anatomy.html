A Tag Wraps Around Content

opening tag closing tag | | v v <p> I like building things. </p> ^ | the content

The rules

  • Tags come in pairs. <p> opens, </p> closes
  • The slash is what makes it a closing tag
  • Tags can sit inside other tags
  • A few tags stand alone, like <img>

Nesting

<p>I like <strong>pizza</strong>.</p>

Close them in the order you opened them. Inside first, outside last.
