05 - HTML

Headings and Paragraphs

<h1>Biggest heading</h1>
<h2>Smaller</h2>
<h3>Smaller still</h3>

<p>
  A paragraph of ordinary text.
</p>

<p>
  A second paragraph.
</p>

h1 through h6

Headings, biggest to smallest. Use one h1 per page, then h2 for each section.

p

A paragraph. Every block of normal text goes in one.

Pressing enter does nothing. HTML ignores your line breaks. If you want a new paragraph, you write a new <p>.
