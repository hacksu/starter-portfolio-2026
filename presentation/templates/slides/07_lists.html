Lists

<ul>
  <li>HTML</li>
  <li>CSS</li>
  <li>Python</li>
</ul>


<ol>
  <li>Write the file</li>
  <li>Open it</li>
  <li>Put it online</li>
</ol>

ul - unordered

Bullet points. Use it when the order does not matter.

ol - ordered

Numbered. Use it for steps, rankings, anything where order carries meaning.

li - list item

One per line in the list. Goes inside ul or ol, never on its own.
