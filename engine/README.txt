This folder is the engine. You can edit these two files, but they are the ones
most likely to break the whole site, and you do not need to touch either of
them to make the site your own.

  global.css      the reset, the navigation, the links, the buttons
  components.js   builds the menu and the footer, and works the Menu button

Everything you actually edit is one folder up:

  index.html      the home page
  projects.html   your projects
  pages.css       your colors, fonts, and layout
  site.js         your name, your menu, your footer line

Want to change what the menu says? That is site.js.
Want to change how the menu looks? That is pages.css.
Want to change how the menu is built? That is components.js, in here.

Do read these two files once the rest makes sense. Reading other people's code
is most of how you learn to write it. Just make a copy first.
